## Direct wording difference

- The 80-page sharh states `فِعَالٌ` as the plural pattern and labels `كُسَالَى` an exception.
- The 62-page sharh explicitly lists both `فِعَالٌ` and `فُعَالَى` as plural patterns for masculine and feminine and adds `سَكْرَانُ/سَكْرَى/سُكَارَى`.
- The public lesson must display both source positions separately and must not silently resolve the difference from outside knowledge.
